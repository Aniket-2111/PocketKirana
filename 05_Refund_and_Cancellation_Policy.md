# Pocket Kirana — Refund & Cancellation Policy
**Effective date:** 12 September 2026

## Cancellation
Where an order has not entered a committed fulfilment stage, cancellation may be requested through the available flow. After processing/dispatch begins, cancellation may be restricted where permitted, subject to mandatory consumer rights.

## Unavailable products
An unavailable item may be removed, substituted with customer approval where supported, or refunded/adjusted.

## Wrong/damaged/eligible product
Contact support promptly with order number, item, issue and evidence where useful. Depending on the case, Pocket Kirana may provide replacement, refund, adjustment or another lawful remedy.

## Food/perishable goods
Return eligibility may depend on safety, freshness, storage and delivery condition. Do not require unsafe food returns.

## Payment failure
If a payment is debited but status is uncertain, reconcile the provider transaction before deciding the final order/payment state.

## COD digital payment
For verified doorstep digital COD payments, refunds should use the verified payment reference and authorised payment/refund process. Delivery partners must not issue unauthorised cash refunds.

## Partial refunds
May be used for unavailable items, approved wrong/damaged items, price corrections or other valid adjustments.

## Refund timing
After approval, the refund is initiated through the relevant payment channel. Actual bank/provider credit time can vary.

## Abuse
Fraudulent or repeated abusive claims may be investigated and accounts may be restricted where lawful.
