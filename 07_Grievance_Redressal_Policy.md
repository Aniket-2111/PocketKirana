## How to complain
Use in-app support, website support or the official support email. Include order number, registered contact, issue category, date/time and payment reference/evidence where relevant.

## Categories
Order, wrong/missing/damaged product, delivery, payment, refund, invoice, account/security, privacy/data, accessibility and other service issues.

## Handling
Complaints should be acknowledged and handled within applicable legal timelines and published service standards. Where resolution is delayed, provide an update where appropriate.

## Privacy complaints
Route privacy/data complaints to the designated privacy contact and handle under the applicable DPDP framework.

## Payment complaints
Reconcile payment complaints against provider records and Pocket Kirana order/payment records. Delivery partners are not the final authority for payment disputes.
