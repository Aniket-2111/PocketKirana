# POCKETKIRANA — DEMO & TEST DATA IDENTIFICATION MANIFEST

**Status:** 🟡 **AUDIT ONLY — STRICT PRESERVATION ACTIVE (ZERO DELETIONS)**  
**Rule:** No database record or mock artifact shall be deleted during Phase 2. Classification is purely diagnostic.

---

## 1. Classification Definitions
- **REAL:** Production-grade seed data, master categories, operational stores, tax rates, sequence definitions, or genuine customer records.
- **DEMO:** Sample catalog items, mock images, test descriptions, placeholder banners, or showcase products intended to illustrate layout.
- **TEST:** Synthetic records generated by test suites, load test runners, or red-team penetration scripts (e.g. `fake_prod_*`, `test_user_*`, `ord-test-*`).
- **UNKNOWN:** Records whose provenance requires verification by the store operations team before promotion or archiving.
