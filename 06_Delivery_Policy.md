# Pocket Kirana — Delivery Policy
**Effective date:** 12 September 2026

## Service area
Delivery is available only in serviceable locations shown by Pocket Kirana.

## Customer information
Customers must provide a complete address, reachable mobile number and useful delivery instructions.

## Delivery time
Displayed times are estimates unless expressly guaranteed. Traffic, weather, stock, order volume, customer availability, payment verification and technical issues may cause delays.

## Handover
Delivery may use OTP or other security verification. Customers should share OTP only at the time of legitimate handover.

## COD digital payment
Where enabled, a transaction-specific payment request/QR may contain the exact order amount. Payment is confirmed only through the authorised provider/backend. A screenshot is not payment proof.

## Already-paid orders
The delivery partner should not collect the order value again when the backend shows verified PAID status.

## Completion
Delivery is completed only after required backend checks, including assignment, payment status where applicable and OTP verification where applicable.

## Location
The delivery app may request location permission for navigation/operational purposes. Location is not the sole proof of delivery.

## Failed delivery
Incorrect address, customer unavailability, refusal or access restrictions may result in a failed/reattempted delivery according to the operational flow.
